Our Story

Driven by a Passion for Simplicity

Banka was founded in 2023 with a single goal: to remove the complexity from banking. We saw a world of confusing interfaces, hidden fees, and legacy systems, and we knew there was a better way. Our founders, a team of financial experts and tech innovators, set out to build a platform that puts people first.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of customers who rely on Banka for its intuitive design, transparent practices, and unwavering commitment to security. We're more than just a bank; we're a partner in your financial journey.

The Values That Guide Us

Our principles are the foundation of our platform and our company culture.

Security First

We are obsessive about security. From multi-factor authentication to end-to-end encryption, we employ industry-leading practices to ensure your money and data are always protected.

Radical Transparency

No hidden fees, no confusing terms. We believe in clear, honest communication and providing you with the information you need to make smart financial decisions.

Customer-Centric Design

Every feature we build is designed with you in mind. We strive to create an experience that is not just functional, but a pleasure to use, regardless of your technical skill level.
