# Client (User) Features

Based on the project specification, the Client role, also referred to as a User, has the following capabilities:

## Authentication
- **Sign Up:** Register for the service using an email address, password, first name, and last name.
- **Email Verification:** Verify their email address by providing a one-time password (OTP) sent to them.
- **Sign In:** Log in to the application using their email and password. Only verified users are allowed to sign in.
- **Forgot Password:** Recover access to their account by resetting their password via an OTP sent to their email.

## Banking Features
- **Create Account:** Create a new bank account after logging in.
- **View Transactions:** View the full transaction history for their account.
- **View Specific Transaction:** View the details of a single, specific transaction.
